编者按：

一个蘑菇如何激发我们对资本主义全球生产链的思考？我们的性别认知来源于生理事实还是社会行为？权力和战争如何隐形地塑造着我们的感知？在“克苏鲁世”，我们怎样与非人物种共生？面对爱这个古老而深奥的哲学问题，我们如何理解它的本质和它在现代社会中的作用？赛博格如何重塑自然？语言与殖民有什么关系？面对全球环境变化，如何结合人文与科学知识重新思考我们与自然的关系？

“15小时”是美国亚利桑那大学东亚系和四川美术学院视觉艺术研究院的师生共同发起的一个阅读项目。15小时是中国重庆市和美国亚利桑那图森市两地的时差，但这样的时差并不会阻隔我们思想的交融。

我们希望能通过阅读与讨论，一起分享有趣的前沿知识，一起探索哲学、艺术、人类学、性别研究、区域研究等领域的国际视野和方法，一起认识当下，一起建立我们对共同世界的共同拥有感。读一本书是读者和作者灵魂的相遇。我们相信通过这样的阅读，可以获取新知、理解世界、认识自己。

读书会将采取线上形式，每一期将安排一位或多位领读人讲解书本内容。欢迎大家的加入，让我们的思想碰撞出更耀眼的火花。

“15 Hours” is an online reading group initiated by students and faculties from the Department of East Asian Studies at the University of Arizona and the College of Visual Art Research at Sichuan Fine Arts Institute. “15 Hours” refers to the time difference betw een the two cities - Chongqing, China and Tucson, Arizona- where the two universities are located. Nonetheless, such a time difference will not hinder conversations.

We aim to share cutting-edge knowledge, explore transnational perspectives and approaches in an interdisciplinary study that involves philosophy, art, anthropology,gender studies,regional studies,among other fields.

We also attempt to understand the present and establish a community of sharing. Since reading is an encounter between the souls of readers and authors, we believe that through a series of reading practices, we can acquire new knowledge, understand the world, and form a better insight of ourselves.

In each session, we will invite one or two leading readers to share their notes. We are looking forward to your participation that must bring us dazzling sparks.

Living Earth Community: Multiple Ways of Being and Knowing

Sam Mickey,Mary Evelyn Tucker,and John Grim

Open Book Publishers, 2020

This anthology examines the interplay between Nature and Culture in the setting of our current age of ecological crisis, stressing the importance of addressing these ecological crises occurring around the planet through multiple perspectives. These perspectives are exemplified through diverse case studies – from the political and ethical implications of thinking with forests, to the capacity of storytelling to motivate action, to the worldview of the Indigenous Okanagan community in British Columbia.

在中国世界的商业化、全球化和城市化背景下研究中国当代艺术，并撰写了诸如艺术家村、创意文化产业、艺术与城市化等主题的文章。目前，她的研究主要集中在东亚的社会参与艺术及其相关类别，如公共艺术、艺术主义和生态艺术。

Dr. Meiqin Wang received her doctorate degree in Art History from the State University of New York at Binghamton and is currently a full professor at California State University, Northridge.

She has researched contemporary art from China in the context of commercialization, globalization, and urbanization of the Chinese world and has written on topics such as artist villages, creative cultural industries, and art and urbanization. Currently, her research focuses on socially engaged art and its related categories, such as public art, artivism, and ecological art in East Asia.

对角线计划，缘起从东北到西南的身体游走，游走构成了中国版图上一条从“草原森林平原”地区到“山地高地”地区的对应线，这条线也与当年胡焕庸的从东北黑河到西南腾冲线呼应。从自然地理到人文经济，从近百年前到当代社会，从农业传统到工业繁荣，我们试图寻找和关联共同的意象、符号、理念与精神，以艺术的形式呈现两个地区的多样风貌，实践与理论结合，并尝试总结一些有效的方法，以促进和影响在地生态的演进。

对角线计划目前已经在中国东北地区的黑龙江省鹤岗市与哈尔滨市，辽宁省沈阳市、抚顺市以及西南地区的四川省成都市、自贡市与重庆市开展以工业与城市为主题的研究与当代艺术创作。
