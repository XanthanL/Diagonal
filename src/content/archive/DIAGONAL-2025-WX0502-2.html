20位来自不同国家及地区的艺术家将在两周的工作和日常交流中，呈现他们的思考、创作、观点。

Twenty artists from different countries and regions will share their work, reflections, and perspectives throughout two weeks of shared practice and daily exchange.

01

2025年的世界正变得不确定，人类世、科技AI，民粹主义，局部冲突以及贸易战争，多重现实的彼此纠葛，世界趋向动荡而保守，跨文化交流对话则变得必要而迫切。本轮RIAW在保留一贯的艺术家自由交流的同时，将更为主动的搭建跨文化对谈的通道，让彼此的境遇能够在对话中获得更多的了解理解，将艺术重新视为一种世界语言，在不断的互动交流之中，探讨各种可能的、积极主动的应对之策。

10位来自不同国家和地区的艺术家与10位来自全国各地的艺术家将共同加入到本轮对话，20位从事不同媒介创作的艺术家将在器·Haus空间工作半个月时间。在这个混杂着现代和古老的环境中，探索及分享不同的生活与艺术经验。

重庆混杂的城市生态系统，一方面，异域的特定环境将提供给艺术家们不同的文化机会，另一方面，注重艺术家创作过程大于作品展示结果的工作坊特质，将打破艺术与日常的惯有界限，对话和互动将成为主体和可能。

The world in 2025 is becoming increasingly uncertain. The entanglement of multiple realities—marked by the Anthropocene, technological AI, populism, localized conflicts, and trade wars—has rendered the world turbulent and conservative. In this context, cross-cultural exchange and dialogue have become both necessary and urgent.

This round of RIAW, while retaining its commitment to free artistic exchange, will proactively build channels for cross-cultural dialogue. Through these conversations, we aim to foster greater understanding of our diverse situations and re-establish art as a universal language. Together, we will explore various proactive strategies for addressing the challenges of our time.

Ten artists from different countries and regions, along with ten artists from across the nation, will join this dialogue. Twenty artists working in various media will spend half a month at ORGANHAUS, exploring and sharing their diverse life and artistic experiences in an environment that blends the modern and the ancient.

Faced with the mixed urban ecosystem of Chongqing, on the one hand, the exotic specific environment will provide artists with different cultural opportunities, and on the other hand, the workshop characteristics that focus on the artist's creative process rather than the display results of their works will break the boundary between art and daily life, and dialogue and interaction will become the subject and possibility.

「2025年瑞器重庆国际艺术家工作坊」为器·Haus空间与瑞器合作项目，由器·Haus团队策划和组织。

背景与动因 Context and Purpose

ORGANHAUS国际艺术家工作坊开始于2008年，是一项强调跨文化对话及在地创作的集体艺术项目，项目设定在两周时间内完成，一半中国艺术家一半国际艺术家共同参与，可视为一项主题导向的短期工作坊与驻地项目的结合。OIAW项目的发起参照了英国三角艺术基金会在国际上通行的类似项目架构，至今已实施了11届，项目强调跨文化对话，强调不同文化语境的艺术家（创作者）共同生活共同工作（吃住行集体行动），同期组织大量的参与者之间的交流对谈，组织相关的在地走访和调研，并最终以集体工作室对公众的开放作为项目的结束。

器·Haus空间上一轮的OIAW项目发生在2018年，我们联合德国杜塞尔多夫城市文化局、星汇当代美术馆共同组织实施，项目以“当媒介成为日常- Questioning Photography Now”为名，将“杜塞尔多夫摄影流派”作为讨论出发点，从“作为日常的摄影/作为媒介的摄影”两个角度展开跨文化对话，获邀参与的中国艺术家包括胡尹萍，李明，黎朗，唐潮，钟云舒，高岩等，德国艺术家则包括Anne Pöhlmann，Martin Denker，Jörg Paul Janka等。

不论从哪方面看，在2025年的当下-世界正变得面目全非-重启本项目将自有其更为复杂的讨论面相及期待或诉求，与之前往届设定明确的讨论主题不同，它是一轮去议题化的交流现场，从事绘画、雕塑、摄影、影像、行为艺术、装置等不同媒介的艺术家获邀参与，设想中的交流将涉及全媒介领域的自由对谈，不同文化背景、不同艺术媒介、不同年龄代际的艺术家，可以在某种临时共同生活的日常性场景中交往交流，艺术在当下世界正不断被稀释挤压（乃或在资本及创意经济的拉扯下整改收编），然，艺术依旧是探究（重塑）世界的重要媒介，对于未来，我们依旧选择 — 相信艺术的力量，相信人的力量。

2025年4月21日至5月3日，我们重庆见！

The ORGANHAUS International Artists' Workshop (OIAW), which began in 2008, is a collective art project that emphasizes intercultural dialogue and site-specific creation. The project is designed to be completed within a two-week period, with an equal number of Chinese and international artists participating together. It can be seen as a combination of a theme-oriented short-term workshop and a residency program.

The initiation of the OIAW project was modeled after similar project structures commonly practiced by the Triangle Arts Foundation in the UK. To date, 11 editions of the project have been implemented. The project emphasizes intercultural dialogue and the coexistence and collaboration of artists (creators) from different cultural contexts (collective living and working, including eating, residing, and traveling together). During the project, numerous exchanges and dialogues among participants are organized, along with related site visits and research. The project concludes with the opening of the collective studio to the public.

The last round of the OIAW project took place in 2018. It was jointly organized with the Düsseldorf City Cultural Bureau and GCA Art Center. The project was titled “When Media Becomes Daily - Questioning Photography Now,” taking the “Düsseldorf School of Photography” as a starting point for discussion. It explored intercultural dialogue from two perspectives: “Photography as Daily Life” and “Photography as Medium.” The Chinese artists invited to participate included Hu Yinping, Li Ming, Li Lang, Tang Chao, Zhong Yunshu, and Gao Yan. The German artists included Anne Pöhlmann, Martin Denker, and Jörg Paul Janka.

No matter which way you look at it, in 2025, the world is changing beyond recognition. Restarting this project will naturally have more complex aspects of discussion, expectations, or demands. Unlike previous editions with clearly defined discussion themes, this round is a de-themed exchange venue. Artists working in different media such as painting, sculpture, photography, video, performance art, and installation are invited to participate. The envisioned exchange will be a free dialogue across all media fields. Artists from different cultural backgrounds, different artistic media, and different generations can interact and communicate in a temporary communal living scenario. Art is constantly being diluted and squeezed in the contemporary world (or even reorganized and absorbed under the pull of capital and the creative economy). However, art remains an important medium for exploring (and reshaping) the world. For the future, we still choose to - believe in the power of art, and believe in the power of people.

See you in Chongqing from April 21 to May 3, 2025!

— 倪昆 Ni Kun

关于器·Haus 空间

器·Haus 空间（Organhaus）是一个由艺术家、策展人共同组成的非营利性独立艺术机构。机构坐落于原重庆北碚北源玻璃厂内，拥有近200平方米的艺术家工作、展示空间及艺术家入驻项目接待室。

Organhaus 致力于试验性综合媒介艺术的探索，定期举办各类艺术展览，并为青年艺术家提供交换工作室空间；接受国际、国内艺术家及艺术机构的艺术交流、驻留计划。机构将依托中国不断开放发展的社会环境，以中国具体的现实语境为对话对象，搭建和尝试连接艺术生产与当代文化之间的书写纽带，自我清理和课题研究，则是现阶段最重要的工作。

ABOUT ORGANHAUS

Organhaus is an artistic organisation initiated and run by artists, curators, researchers, and cultural practitioners themselves. It functions as an experimental platform, composing and framing its own body of space together with its friends, visitors, residents, and caretakers, to facilitate a workplace that is both grounded in and constantly revising the contradictory interplay between the autonomous context of aesthetic practice and the socio-historical condition of cultural production.

Conjoining residency programmes and self-initiated curatorial projects as its main trajectory, Organhaus is open to practitioners working across diverse media, disciplines and cultural contexts who are motivated to move beyond the current institutional and market-driven frameworks in the fields of art and culture. It is a place where exhibition-making and event production are engaged as temporary laboratories, from which inquiry and exploration unfold and mediate transformation.

Founded in 2006 by Yang Shu and Ni Kun, the space was originally located at the 501 Art Base in Chongqing before relocating to a defunct facility of the Beibei Glass Factory in 2023. It now includes four shared studios spanning 250 square meters, seven bedrooms, and a 100-square-meter exhibition space with an open kitchen.
