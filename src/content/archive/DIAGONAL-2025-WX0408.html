《失温Hypothermy》

Publish2025.04.08 12:22 SourceWeChat / 对角线计划Diagonal LocationON / Online BylineGOYA ART

在俄罗斯西伯利亚勘察加半岛与哈尔滨之间寻找温度

《失温》是一场关于记忆、迁徙与身份认同的影像探索。艺术家荒草 将镜头对准了俄罗斯西伯利亚的勘察加半岛和中国东北的哈尔滨，这两个在地理上相距遥远，却在历史与文化上紧密相连的地方。

“失温”不仅指代着两地严酷的自然环境，更隐喻着个体在时代洪流中的迷失与疏离。艺术家通过个人化的影像记录，捕捉了勘察加半岛原始荒凉的生活情境与哈尔滨城市生活的烟火气息，在对比与碰撞中，探寻着人类与自然、历史与现实、记忆与身份之间的复杂关系。

Hypothermyis a visual exploration of memory, migration and identity through images. Artist Ray Cheung focuses his lens on the Kamchatka Peninsula in Siberia, Russia and Harbin in Northeast China, two regions that are geographically distant but closely linked in history and culture.

Hypothermynot only represents the harsh natural environment of the regions, but also metaphorically implies the confusion and estrangement of individuals in the torrent of the times. Through photo documentationwith a distinguished personal style, the artist captures the primitive and desolate living conditions of the Kamchatka Peninsula and the bustling life of the city of Harbin. In the contrast and collision, he explores the complex relationships between humans and nature, history and reality, memory and identity.

艺术家荒草，用朴实的镜头对准生活在这片土地上的人们，展现在自然力量的作用下生命的韧性。

将勘察加与哈尔滨的影像并置，通过对比、呼应、隐喻等手法，引发观众对两地历史、文化、身份认同等问题的思考。

With afocus on the people living on theselands,RayCheung’s simple and unadorned shots,manifestingthe resilience of life under the natural conditions.

By juxtaposing the images of Kamchatka and Harbin, and through techniques such as contrast, echoing and metaphor, the series makes the audience give thought to issues such as the history, culture and identity of the two regions.

* **独特的主题视角：** 以个人化的影像记录，连接起勘察加与哈尔滨两个看似遥远的地方，展现两地之间微妙的历史文化联系。

Through photo documentation with a distinguished personal style, this project connects Khabarovsk and Harbin, two seemingly distant regions with subtle historical and cultural connections.

* **强烈的视觉冲击：** 色彩浓度的强烈对比，荒凉与繁华的碰撞，营造出强烈的视觉冲击力，引发观众的情感共鸣。

The intense contrast of color intensities and the collision between desolation and prosperity, create a powerful visual impact, evoking the emotional resonance of the audience.

* **深刻的思考空间：** 展览不仅是对两地的记录，更是对人类生存状态、文化记忆、身份认同等问题的深刻思考。

Not ameredocumentation, the series is also a profound reflection on issues such as the human existence, cultural memory and identity recognition.

艺术家：荒草

荒草：来自中国北京的人文摄影师，长期创作人群与社会及环境共生关系的主题项目。受到经典纪实叙事方式的影响，习惯通过对等的观察去拍摄，用平静视觉方式呈现人群与所处社会环境的关联与碰撞，通过冷静的语言去传递观点与探讨。

Ray Cheung: A Chinese photographer from Beijing who has been engaged in long-term projects focusing on the symbiotic relationship between people, society and the environment. Influenced by classic documentary narrative methods, he is accustomed to shooting through reciprocal observation, depicting the connections and collisions between people and the social environments in a undisturbed visual way, conveying viewpoints and exploring issues through calm language.

策展人：滕玥

滕玥：哈尔滨人，旅法策展人。在中国、美国和法国的人文和社会科学求学经历带给她对跨文化主题的热爱和敏锐度。喜欢用全球化的角度剖析与对比文化元素、社会现象与艺术生态。在巴黎和北京分别运营策展机构BI.ART和直线距离文化Haversine。

Cecilia Yue Teng: Born in Harbin, Cecilia is a curator based in France. Her academic backgrounds in humanities and social sciences in China, the United States, and France have endowed her with a passion and keenness for cross-cultural themes. She enjoys analyzing and comparing cultural elements, social phenomena, and the ecosystem ofartand culture from a global perspective. She respectively operates the twocuratorialagencies,BI.ART Paris, andHaversine CultureBeijing.
