开幕倒计时｜第 12 届 UP-ON 向上国际现场艺术节

Location: ON / Online Publish: 2024.10.17 18:51

阿胖艺术节工作组

16年，11届，246位国内外艺术家参加，众多同道支持赞助，让始于2008年的UP-ON向上走到今天，成为在国际现场艺术领域有良好口碑的艺术节。

纵然现实不易，前路难卜，只管同道携手，以梦为马，不负昭华。

——周斌

UP-ON向上国际现场艺术节总监

In 16 years, the UP-ON International Live Art Festival has held a total of 11 editions, featuring 246 domestic and international artists. Along the way, it has received support and sponsorship from many peer institutions, allowing UP-ON, which began in 2008, to reach this point today and become a well-regarded festival in the field of international live art.

Despite the challenges of reality and the uncertainties ahead, let us join hands with like-minded individuals, using dreams as our steed, and live up to our aspirations.

——ZHOU BIN

Director of UP-ON International Live Art Festival

艺术节工作组：UP-ON向上艺术节已举办了11届，组织方式和所呈现的作品有什么样的发展和变化？

周斌：艺术节的组织方式没有大的变化，在细节上不断完善。艺术节不设主题，这是出于在艺术节上尽可能呈现丰富的艺术面貌的考虑。纵观12届艺术节的作品，可以清晰地体现出国际行为艺术领域在学术方向和创作方法语言上的发展变化。特别是近年来随着中国艺术家视野和经历的开拓与丰富，作品的面貌日趋多样化和国际化。很多精彩的作品很难用语言描述，需要来创作现场观摩体会。

Festival Working Team: The UP-ON Art Festival has held 11 editions. What developments and changes have occurred in its organization and the works presented?

Zhou Bin: The organizational approach of the festival hasn’t changed significantly, but we continuously refine the details. We don’t set a theme for the festival to showcase a diverse range of artistic expressions. Over the 12 editions, the works clearly reflect the evolution of international performance art in terms of academic direction and creative methodologies. Particularly in recent years, as Chinese artists expand their horizons and experiences, the works have become increasingly diverse and internationalized. Many remarkable pieces are challenging to describe in words; experiencing them live is essential.

卓梦婷：请问你如何看待现场行为与档案的关系？

周斌：相辅相成的关系，相互不可替代，当然现场才是核心，是作品本身。行为艺术作品的现场结束后，未来的展示、研究都需要文献档案去还原。早期有些艺术家出于反商业的角度，拒绝记录，这是特例。

Mengting：How do you view the relationship between live performance and its archival records?

Zhou Bin: They complement each other and are mutually irreplaceable; of course, the live performance is the core, the essence of the artwork itself. After the live performance of a work of art concludes, future presentations and research need documentation to reconstruct it. In the early days, some artists, motivated by anti-commercial perspectives, refused to document their work, but that was an exception.

卓梦婷：举办现场艺术节涉及到许多资源调配，艺术节是如何在资源有限的情况下实现可持续发展的？

周斌：UP-ON向上艺术节的四点坚持是：纯学术、国际化、公共性、非营利。首先是努力做出高质量的艺术节，好的口碑，才能得道多助。非营利的性质也很重要，每届艺术节结束后账目都会对外公示。艺术节自2008年走到今天，16年举办了11届，一路上得到了太多机构和个人的人力物力支持，是吃着百家饭长大的，每届艺术节的繁杂工作也都是由众多认可艺术节价值的志愿者以爱发电，合力完成的。

Mengting：Given that performance art can be resource-intensive, how does the festival manage to sustain itself and continue growing while working within these constraints?

Zhou Bin: The four principles of the UP-ON International Live Art Festival are: academic purity, internationalization, public accessibility, and non-profit status. First and foremost, we strive to create a high-quality art festival; a good reputation attracts more support. The non-profit nature is also crucial, as the accounts are publicly disclosed after each festival. Since its inception in 2008, the festival has hosted 11 editions over 16 years, receiving extensive support from various organizations and individuals. The complex tasks of each festival are accomplished collaboratively by numerous volunteers who believe in the festival’s value and are fueled by their passion.

卓梦婷：艺术节做了哪些事情实现国际与本地艺术家之间有效的交流？

周斌：艺术节长达十天，期间会通过多种方式为大家搭建交流平台：现场创作、工作坊、讲座和分享会。艺术节邀请艺术家的学术门槛比较高，也是为了保障大家在专业层面能够同频有效地交流。

Mengting：What initiatives have been undertaken to facilitate meaningful exchange between international and local artists?

Zhou Bin: The festival lasts for ten days, during which we create various platforms for exchange: live creation, workshops, lectures, and sharing sessions. We set a high academic threshold for invited artists to ensure that communication occurs effectively professionally.

卓梦婷：成都的环境如何与艺术节相辅相成？

周斌：成都本身文化土壤相对良好，包容、开放。从上世纪九十年代开始，我们也一直在有意识地构建着适合行为艺术发展的生态环境。加上艺术节自身的生命力顽强，所以阿胖今年16岁了。

Mengting：How does the environment of Chengdu complement and interact with the festival?

Zhou Bin: Chengdu has a relatively rich cultural soil, being inclusive and open. Since the 1990s, we have consciously been building an ecological environment conducive to the development of performance art. Coupled with the festival's own resilience, it’s thriving; after all, it has turned 16 this year.

UP-ON采访问题部分由卓梦婷编撰。

卓梦婷是现居伦敦/广州两地的现场艺术家和策展人，也是艺术播客《小明拆台》的创始人之一。

The UP-ON interview questions were compiled by Zhuo Mengting, a live artist and curator based between London and Guangzhou. She is also a co-founder and host of the arts podcast Ming Strike.

主办机构

HOST ORGANISATION

UP-ON向上行为艺术档案馆（UP-ON PAA）是聚焦行为艺术研究推广的独立非营利机构。以国际视野收集保存行为艺术资料文献，有针对性地对其进行梳理、展示和研究。亦通过组织讲座、论坛、工作坊、艺术节、出版等多种形式的活动，搭建专业化的交流实践平台。

档案馆强调自身不是一个静态的文献资料存储空间，而是一个发动机和孵化器，是推动行为艺术当下实践和探讨未来方向的平台。

The UP-ON Performance Art Archive (UP-ON PAA) is an independent non-profit organization focused on the research and promotion of performance art. Coming from an international perspective, the Archive collects and preserves performance art materials and documents, working diligently on the culling, presentation, and research of these archives. UP-ON PAA also organizes lectures, forums, workshops, art festivals, and publications with a view to building a professional platform for the communication and practice of performance art.

The Archive highlights itself not as a static storage space for documentary materials, but as an engine, incubator, and platform for promoting the current practice of performance art, whilst also exploring its future trajectories.

联合主办机构

CO-HOST ORGANISATIONS

A4美术馆是致力于“用艺术连接人与人，激活创造力”的非营利美术馆集群。持续推动具有创造力的多元艺术生态发展，让更多人参与艺术创造，为公众带来丰富的高品质当代艺术展览、公共项目和研究计划。

A4美术馆于2008年由成都万华投资集团创办成立，2023年A4美术馆主馆从麓湖迁址到麓镇。在创始馆长孙莉的带领下，A4美术馆持续建设着西南艺术生态发展，拓展了国际与在地社区的联动、推动社区艺术的发展；关注于社会公平、公共参与、社会化教育等方向，创立了关注于儿童和教育公共性的年度项目。

A4美术馆集群目前包含四个品牌：位于麓镇山顶广场的A4美术馆（A4 Art Museum），位于麓湖艺展中心的A4儿童馆（A4 Kids）、A4国际驻留艺术中心（A4 Residency Art Center）和A4X 艺术中心（A4 X Art Center）。在未来，A4美术馆将持续以人为核心，通过艺术展览和公共项目，使艺术真正融入日常生活，让更多人因为艺术的力量产生更紧密的连接，成为伙伴，共同创造属于彼此的美术馆社区。

A4 Art Museum is a non-profit museum group dedicated to connecting people through art and activating creativity. The museum promotes the development of a creative and diverse art ecosystem, so that more people can participate in creating art. The museum brings viewers varied, high-quality contemporary art exhibitions, public programs, and research projects. A4 Art Museum was founded in 2008 by Chengdu Wide Horizon Investment Co., Ltd. and the main A4 Art Museum moved from Luxelakes to Luxetown in 2023. Led by founding director Sunny Sun, A4 Art Museum has continued to develop the art scene in southwestern China, expand connections between international and local communities, and promote the development of community art. The museum has launched annual children’s and public education programs with an emphasis on social justice, public participation, and socialized education. The new A4 Art Museum group encompasses four brands: A4 Art Museum at Luxetown’s Mountaintop Plaza, A4 Kids in the Luxelakes Art Expo Center, A4 Residency, and A4:X Art Center. In the future, A4 Art Museum will continue to focus on people. Through art exhibitions and public programs, the museum brings art into everyday life and encourages closer connections between people through the power of art, so that visitors become partners and co-creators in the building of a museum community that belongs to everyone.

近悦儿童美术馆于2023年2月落成开馆，主张“Childhood is for everybody, Children are the future”，从童年走向未来的理念，以当代艺术的视角，将“大儿童范畴”作为课题和对象来研究，通过主题展览、学术论坛、研究出版、公共项目等多维度形式，为全年龄段群体搭建一个多元的艺术交流平台，通过当代艺术让社会更充分地认识和理解儿童。我们的研究主题从儿童延展到全社会，进而尝试提供一个更健康、有益的社会环境。

Jinyue Children's Art Museum opened in February , 2023 with the concept of Childhood is for everybody, Children are the future. From the perspective of contemporary art, takes 'the category of childhood' as the subject and object of research. Through thematic exhibitions, academic forums, research and publication, public projects and other multi-dimensional forms, it builds a diversified artistic exchange platform for all age groups, and enables the society to know and understand children more fully through contemporary art. Our researchs' themes from children extend to whole society and try to provide a healthier and more beneficial society environment.

NY20+位于四川成都，是浓园文化旗下专业的国际文化艺术交流推广机构。浓园文化拥有三个主题艺术园区，总面积达150,000平方米。依托浓园的艺术+自然生态聚落和200多位在地艺术家资源， NY20+以驻留项目、展览策划、校企合作和公益平台为核心，以推动不同国家、地区间的文化艺术个体与群体之间的互动与合作，促进不同文化的传播、碰撞与发展，实现跨国艺术生态的良性循环和艺术文化生物链的良性发展。

NY20+ is a Sichuan-based international art organization. It is supported by Nongyuan Culture which fosters unique art and ecological environment with three theme art Zones covering about 150,000 square meters in total, as well as more than 200 resident Chinese artists. With artist residency program, exhibition planning, school-enterprise cooperation and non-profit art platform at its core, it is committed to strengthening interaction between art individual and community from various countries and regions, facilitating the dissemination, collision and development of diverse cultures, and thus realizing the virtuous development of transnational art and culture ecology.

UP-ON向上国际现场艺术节

四点坚持:

THE FOUR POINTS OF THE UP-ON INTERNATIONAL LIVE ART FESTIVAL

● 非营利 UNCOMMERCIALLY

艺术节无营利目的，工作团队除在艺术活动期间有与艺术家同等的补贴外，其余时间为无薪酬工作。艺术节结束后半月向社会公开财务账目。

The festival is organized without profit-making purposes, and the staff works without pay, except during artistic activities when they receive subsidies, just like the artists. The financial accounts are made public within two weeks after the festival concludes.

● 国际化 INTERNATIONALIZATION

艺术节创办伊始就定位于国际化的专业交流平台。广泛邀请世界各地的艺术家参与，他们中既有从上世纪七十年代就从事现场艺术创作的前辈，也有充满冒险精神的新锐。

The festival has been set up as an international platform for professional exchanges since the beginning. Artists worldwide are invited to participate in the festival, including those engaged in live art creation since the 1970s and newcomers full of adventurous spirit.

● 学术性 ACADEMICNATURE

艺术节努力邀请到国内外创作经验丰富的优秀艺术家参与，并促成近距离的专业性的学术交流和分享，对国际范围内现场艺术最前沿的课题展开对话和探讨。

The festival strives to invite the participation of outstanding artists with rich creative experience at home and abroad, facilitates close and professional academic exchanges and sharing, and initiates dialogues and discussions on the most cutting-edge topics of live art on an international scale.

● 公共性 PUBLICITY

艺术节期间，艺术家们除创作作品外，还会在一些艺术机构、社区和大学举办讲座、工作坊等多种形式的活动，促进和提升公众对现场艺术的认知。

During the festival, artists will create works and hold lectures, workshops, and other activities in some art organizations, communities, and universities to promote and enhance public awareness of live art.

作为非营利性质的专业活动， UP-ON向上国际现场艺术艺术节能举办到第十二届，得益于众多同道多年来的持续支持。这包括国内外艺术家的敬业创作，志愿者、工作组团队的倾力付出，业内人士的献计鞭策，众多合作机构从经费、场地、人员等多方面的资助，还少不了的是公众的积极参与。靠吃百家饭，纳百家福成长起来的UP-ON向上艺术节，自知要以如履薄冰的心态，以国际视野自我审视，不懈提升专业品质，方能不负大家的帮扶。来日方长，同道砥砺前行。

UP-ON International Live Art Festival can be held for the twelfth year as a non-profit professional event, thanks to the continuous support of many fellow artists over the years. This includes the dedication of domestic and foreign artists, the dedication of volunteers and working groups, the advice of industry insiders, the financial support of many partner organizations in terms of funding, venues, personnel, etc., and not least, the active participation of the public. UP-ON International Live Art Festival, which grows by eating a hundred meals and enjoying a hundred blessings, knows that it has to walk on thin ice, self-examine with an international vision, and unremittingly improve its professional quality to live up to everyone's support. The future is long, and it is the same way to move forward.
