前言 PREFACE

“浮游”剧场介绍 （ Introduction Of Floating Theater）

“浮游”剧场是“昨天今天明天”剧场续集，“浮游”暗含双重隐喻：既是水中浮游生物——生态链中最渺小却不可或缺的基础生命体；又喻指人类在权力网络中的悬浮状态——既被规训体制所捕获，又试图在缝隙中保持轻盈的游动姿态。这一命名与福柯笔下的“全景敞视监狱”形成深刻对话，揭示现代人生存的悖论性境遇。

福柯的“全景敞视监狱”理论指出，现代社会通过空间分配、层级监视和规范化裁决，将个体驯化为“驯服而有用”的身体。而“浮游”恰是对此的抵抗意象——浮游生物无固定轨迹，在海洋中被动漂流却维系着整个生态系统的存续。正如剧场中的演员与观众，虽身处规训空间（如固定的座位、舞台的凝视结构），却可能通过即兴表演打破预设脚本，以身体的“不可预测性”松动权力的网格。

剧场本质是福柯所称的“异托邦”（heterotopia）——一个真实社会中被隔离的、兼具反射与对抗功能的空间。在中文版《浮士德》中，图米纳斯导演以“欢庆生命”对抗苦难：当尹昉饰演的靡菲斯特从“狗形”蜕变为魔鬼时，肢体语言解构了角色的固有符号；当工人推动巨型书柜碾压舞台时，知识体系的崩塌成为对规训的赤裸嘲讽。剧场由此成为“全景敞视”的反面：监视塔的视线被舞台的黑暗吞噬，观众席的集体凝视反而转化为共谋式的解放——人们在此窥见规训的暴力，也重获“自我呵护”的可能。

现代人犹如社会水域中的浮游生物：一面被“绩效理性”的巨网捕捞，一面在生态崩溃与技术异化中飘摇。福柯的深刻洞见在于：权力不仅压抑，更生产主体性；反抗始于“成为自己所不是”的实践。阿来在《大河源》中书写黄河源头的普氏原羚与光伏板下的羊群，提出“人类仅是生态网络的节点”——这恰是“浮游剧场”的终极启示：承认渺小方能超越傲慢，在共生中寻找规训外的呼吸孔道。

“浮游剧场”是献给规训社会的一则存在主义寓言。当福柯断言“灵魂是身体的监狱”时，剧场以疯癫的欢庆回应：若理性是另一种疯癫，那么艺术便是第三种理性——它让我们在权力的可见性深渊中，以浮游之姿托起生命的重量。

"Plane" is a sequel to "Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow," and "plane" implies a dual metaphor: plankton in water - the smallest but indispensable basic living organism in the ecological chain; It is also a reference to human beings' floating state in power networks - both captured by the discipline system and trying to remain lightly mobile in the gaps. This designation forms a profound dialogue with Foucault's "Panoramic View of the Prison," which reveals the paradoxical circumstances of modern human existence. Foucault's "panoramic prison" theory states that modern society domesticates individuals into "tameable and useful" bodies through space distribution, hierarchical surveillance, and normative adjudication. Plankton is an image of resistance to this - plankton without a fixed trajectory, floating passively in the ocean, but maintaining the survival of the entire ecosystem. Just as actors and viewers in theaters, although in a disciplined space (such as fixed seating, a staring structure on the stage), they may break preset scripts through improvisation, loosening the grid of power through physical "unpredictability." The essence of theatre is what Foucault calls "heterotopia" - an isolated, reflective and antagonistic space in real society. In the Chinese version of Faust, director Tuminis confronts suffering by "celebrating life": body language deconstructs the character's inherent symbolism as Yin Fan, played by Mi Faust, transforms from "dog-like" to devil. The collapse of the body of knowledge makes a naked mockery of discipline as workers push huge bookcases over the stage. The theater thus becomes the opposite of "full view": the view of the surveillance tower is swallowed by the darkness of the stage, and the collective gaze of the audience turns into a conspiratorial liberation - a place where people peer into the violence of discipline and regain the possibility of "self-preservation." Modern people are like plankton in the social waters: they are caught in the huge net of "performance-based reason" on the one hand, on the other hand, they are drifting in the ecological collapse and alienation of technology. Foucault's profound insight was that power was not only repressive, but also productive. Rebellion begins with the practice of being what you are not. In Big Heyuan, Alai wrote about the Przewalski's gazelle at the source of the Yellow River and the sheep under the photovoltaic panel. He proposed that "human beings are only the nodes of the ecological network" - this is the ultimate inspiration of the "floating theater": recognizing the small can go beyond the arrogant, and find the breathing channel outside the discipline in the symbiosis. "The Planned Theater" is an existential fable dedicated to disciplined society. When Foucault asserted that "the soul is the prison of the body," the theater responded with a mad rejoicing: if reason is another mad rejoinder, then art is a third kind of reason - one that allows us to carry the weight of life in the visible abyss of power.

剧本介绍 （Description Of The Script）

在一片浩瀚的海洋里，这里有一个不足5平米的小岛，所有的动物和谐相处，那里蓝色的海洋与蓝天像极了蓝色宝石，空气中充满着浓郁的泥土和芳香。

在都市逃离出来的人他放弃了都市的一切，他成了这个岛唯一的居民，他开始重新学习如何生存和生活，与这里花草树木、飞禽走兽、海风阳光等等的日常简单的生活成了他最快乐的时光。

突然有一天这里电闪雷鸣，乌云密布，海水倒灌，森林大火…………

In a vast ocean, there is a small island of less than 5 square meters where all the animals live in harmony; the blue ocean and the blue sky look like blue gems, and the air is filled with the rich scent of the soil and fragrant flowers.

A person who has escaped the hustle and bustle of the city has abandoned everything in the city and has become the only inhabitant of this island. He has started to relearn how to survive and live, and the simple daily life with the flowers, trees, birds, animals, sea breeze, sunshine, and so on has become his happiest time.

One day, there was a sudden flash of lightning, dark clouds gathered, seawater overflowed, and a forest fire broke out...

艺术家简介

Bio of Artist

甘志鹏 Gan Zhipeng

1996年9月生于甘肃平凉，四川美术学院硕士，现生活工作于四川成都。他的作品涉及绘画、行为、剧场、表演、影像等创作方式，聚焦于内心世界与现实之间的矛盾关系关注个体精神在现实困境中的处境和生存状态，以及个体与现实语境中的无力感、荒诞性和戏剧性，试图在这个“边缘缝隙”之中试图完成一种“不确定价值”的延伸。受安托南·阿尔托（Antonin Artaud）“残酷戏剧”的理念影响，近期热衷于“剧场”、“表演”、“身体性”的研究与探索，强调剧场的仪式和身体体验的本质，在追寻“身体性”表达的同时试图找到行为和戏剧表演的“交叉点”。

近期个展/剧场项目/现场作品：《昨天 今天 明天》实验剧场，成都·红印艺术中心，2024年，四川成都，中国；《出离》，“呓勃會”行为现场艺术节第四回，2024年，福建漳州，中国；《平行线》实验剧场，501艺术基地-序空间，2023年，重庆黄桷坪，中国；《小说》行为剧场，FUN·艺术空间，“循环”在云上国际现场行为艺术节，2023年，云南昆明，中国；《我是一个好人》，“触发”-周斌行为艺术结课展，麓湖·A4美术馆，2023年，四川成都，中国；《天人合一》，第十七回“谷雨行动”国际行为艺术节，2023年，陕西西安，中国；《无题》行为剧场，HE·基地·临时美术馆，2022年，重庆黄桷坪，中国。

Gan Zhipeng (b.1996), master of Sichuan Academy of Fine Arts, now living and working in Chengdu, Sichuan. His work involves painting, performance, theater, performance, and images, focusing on the spear-shield relationship between the inner world and reality, which focuses on the situation and existence of the individual spirit in real dilemmas. And the powerlessness, absurdity, and drama of the individual and the context of reality attempt to accomplish an extension of an "uncertain value" within this "edge gap." Influenced by the concept of "cruel theater" by Antonin Artaud, I have recently been enthusiastic about researching and exploring "theater," "performance," and "physicality," emphasizing the essence of ritual and physical experience in theater, and seeking to find the "intersection" between behavior and theatrical performance while pursuing the expression of "physicality."

Recent theater projects and live productions: 《Yesterday,Today,Tomorrow"》Experimental Theater, Chengdu Red Print Art Center, 2024, Chengdu, Sichuan Province, China; 《Detachment》，" Balderdash Live Art Festival 4th, 2024, Zhangzhou, Fujian, China; 《Parallel lines》Experimental Theatre, 501 Art Base - Xu Space, 2023, Huangjueping, Chongqing, China;《Novel 》Performance Theatre, FUN · art space, "Circulation" on the cloud international live performance art festival, 2023, Kunming, Yunnan, China; 《I am a Good Man》， "Trigger" - Zhou Bin's Art Exhibition, Luxhu · A4 Art Museum, 2023, Chengdu, Sichuan, China; 《Heaven and Man in One》，17th "Grain Rain Operation" International Performance Festival, 2023, Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, China;《Untitled》Performance Theatre, HE · Base · Temporary Art Gallery, 2022, Huangping, Chongqing, China.
